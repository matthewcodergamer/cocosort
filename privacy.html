CocoSort Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

CocoSort ("the game", "we") is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information is collected when you play and why.

Information the game itself keeps

Your progress (current level, coins, lives, settings and daily-puzzle history) is saved only on your device. We do not run accounts or servers, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which helps keep it free. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be inferred
  • App interactions with ads, and diagnostic and performance information

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Learn more about how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Coin packs, bundles and No Ads are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the game only receives a confirmation of the order to deliver the item, and never sees your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
