Privacy Policy

Cocosort: Tropical Water Sort · Android app (com.cocosort.game) · Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short: Cocosort is a single-player puzzle game. We do not run accounts or servers, and we do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows advertising through Google AdMob, and AdMob processes device and advertising identifiers in order to serve and measure those ads. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before personalised advertising is used.

1. Who we are

The data controller for the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Zixxq Core Studios (“we”, “us”, “our”)
Developer of Cocosort: Tropical Water Sort
Contact: agooddclawyer@gmail.com

This policy explains what information is collected when you install and play the game, why it is collected, how long it is kept, and the rights you have over it.

2. Information the game stores on your device

The game keeps the following on your device only, in the app’s private storage. It is not transmitted to us and we cannot read it:

  • your progress (current level, completed levels);
  • in-game currency, lives and boosters;
  • your settings and preferences (sound, music, haptics, language);
  • your daily-puzzle history.

This data is deleted when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the game. We have no ability to access, recover or delete it for you remotely, because it never leaves your device.

3. Information collected by advertising and platform services

The game is free and is supported by advertising delivered through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. To deliver, cap, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • Advertising and device identifiers — such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID), app set ID, and general device information (device model, manufacturer, screen size, operating system version, system language, mobile network code, country).
  • IP address — which may be used to infer an approximate (city or region level) location. We do not collect or request precise GPS location.
  • Advertising interaction data — which ads were requested, shown, clicked or closed, and whether an install or conversion was attributed.
  • Diagnostic and performance data — crash logs, SDK error reports, latency and fraud-prevention signals.
  • Consent signals — your consent choices, encoded in the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) string, so that partners know what they may and may not do with your data.

We do not receive this data in identifiable form. Google provides us only with aggregated reports (for example, total ad impressions and estimated revenue).

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (coin packs, bundles, “No Ads”) are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment method and card details; we never see them. We receive only a purchase confirmation and an order identifier so the item can be delivered and restored on your device.

What we never collect

We do not collect your name, postal address, email address, phone number, contacts, calendar, photos, files, SMS, call logs, microphone or camera content, health data, biometric data, or precise geolocation. The game does not require you to create an account.

5. Consent, and how to change or withdraw it

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region with similar rules, the game shows a consent message powered by Google’s User Messaging Platform the first time ads are requested. That message lists the advertising partners that may process your data and lets you accept, reject, or manage purposes and vendors individually, in line with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2).

  • Personalised advertising is used only if you consent.
  • If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalised: selected using contextual information and limited device data, not your advertising profile.
  • You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from the game’s Settings → Privacy options (or “Ad privacy settings”) entry, which reopens the consent form. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

You can also limit ad personalisation at the operating-system level:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (Android 12+) or Opt out of Ads Personalisation (older versions).
  • Resetting your advertising ID does not delete data already collected, but it disconnects future activity from the previous identifier.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own independent marketing. Data is shared only with the service providers needed to run the game:

  • Google AdMob / Google LLC — advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and consent management. See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Google advertising partners — the third-party ad technology providers disclosed in the consent message and listed by Google at AdMob’s authorised partners page. Each partner acts under its own privacy policy and receives only the data permitted by the consent you gave.
  • Google Play Billing — processing in-app purchases (Google Privacy Policy).
  • GitHub, Inc. — hosting this policy page; GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you load it (GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • Authorities — where we are legally required to disclose information, or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

If ownership of the game changes, data may be transferred to the successor as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

7. International transfers

Google is a global company, so data may be processed outside the EEA/UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards: the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified. You can ask us for more information about the safeguards used.

8. How long we keep information

  • On-device game data — kept until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the game.
  • Advertising data — retained by Google and its partners according to their own retention schedules (typically up to 14 months for ad-serving logs, and longer in aggregated or de-identified form). We do not hold a separate copy.
  • Purchase records — held by Google Play; we keep only the minimum needed to honour the purchase and meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • Support correspondence — kept for as long as needed to resolve your query, then deleted.

9. Security

We keep the game free of accounts and servers, which minimises the data at risk. Data in transit between the app and Google’s advertising services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we and our providers apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, including access controls, pseudonymisation and fraud detection.

10. Your rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erase your data (“right to be forgotten”);
  • Restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests and processing for direct marketing;
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out;
  • Not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not carry out such decision-making;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk).

To exercise any of these rights, email agooddclawyer@gmail.com. We respond within one month, and may extend that by two further months for complex requests, in which case we will tell you. We may need to ask for information to confirm your identity, but because we hold very little data about you, in many cases the fastest route is to delete the app’s data and reset or delete your advertising ID, and to exercise ad-related choices directly through the consent form or Google’s ad settings.

If you are a California resident, or a resident of another US state with a comprehensive privacy law (for example Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or Utah), you have the right to:

  • Know what categories of personal information are collected, the sources, the purposes, and the categories of third parties they are shared with;
  • Access and obtain a copy of that information;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Delete information collected from you;
  • Opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, including sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising;
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information — we do not collect sensitive personal information;

The categories of information involved are identifiers (advertising ID, IP address) and internet or other network activity (ad interactions), collected for advertising and analytics purposes as described above. We do not knowingly sell personal information for money. Sharing with advertising partners for cross-context behavioural advertising may be considered “sharing” or a “sale” under some state laws; you can opt out by declining consent in the consent form, by using the “Privacy options” entry in the game’s settings, or by emailing us. We honour opt-out preference signals, including Global Privacy Control, where we are technically able to receive them.

12. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in some EU member states), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are under that age, please do not play, or play only with a parent or guardian’s permission and supervision. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Where an app is configured for a child-directed audience, AdMob serves only non-personalised, context-appropriate ads. If you believe the game is being used by a child, you can also enable Google Play’s Family Link controls on the device.
